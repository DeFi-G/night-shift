# Night Shift V15

## Hacksgiving 2026 MVP

Developed by DeFi Network.

Night Shift is a cloud-hosted autonomous assignment worker
designed to investigate uploaded materials, collect evidence,
perform bounded analytical operations, and deliver reports.

Built using Fly.io Sprites and Ollama intelligence integration.

## Capabilities

- Detached assignment execution.
- Evidence-backed investigation.
- Document inspection and search.
- Deterministic CSV analytics and calculations.
- Original-file integrity verification.
- Bounded inference spending.
- Persistent assignment status and reports.
- Discord clock-in and completion notifications.
- Desktop and mobile browser interface.
- Explicit authorization and execution controls.

## Release selection

V15 was selected over the experimental V16 candidate.

V16 completed three of five assignments and consumed eleven
inference requests, with an estimated inference cost of
USD 0.00380880.

V15 was selected for its higher observed completion rate
and lower inference consumption in this limited evaluation.

The owner approved V15 as the official Hacksgiving release
on November 27, 2026.

## Known limitations

- Qualification covers a limited five-assignment sample.
- Completed reports are not necessarily fully accurate.
- Model-generated conclusions require human review.
- Deterministic analytical verification does not independently
  certify every statement in a generated report.
- Live inference requires explicit authorization and configuration.
- Production operation has not been authorized.
- This is a hackathon MVP, not a production-certified service.
